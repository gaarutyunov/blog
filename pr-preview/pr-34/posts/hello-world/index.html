Welcome to the new home of my writing. I rebuilt this blog from the ground up on Astro, wired to my own ga-ui-kit — a zero-dependency set of native Web Components that also powers garutyunov.com.

Why the rewrite

The old site had accumulated cruft and content I no longer stand behind. Starting clean lets me:

  • keep the design language consistent with my portfolio,
  • own the whole stack with a component kit I control, and
  • publish a proper RSS feed so garutyunov.com can surface the latest posts.

What’s next

More posts on the things I build — hardware, firmware, tools, and the occasional detour. Subscribe via RSS if that’s your thing.
