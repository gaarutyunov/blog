Welcome to the new home of my writing. I rebuilt this blog from the ground up on
[Astro](https://astro.build), wired to my own
[ga-ui-kit](https://github.com/gaarutyunov/ui-kit) — a zero-dependency set of
native Web Components that also powers [garutyunov.com](https://garutyunov.com).

## Why the rewrite

The old site had accumulated cruft and content I no longer stand behind. Starting
clean lets me:

- keep the design language consistent with my portfolio,
- own the whole stack with a component kit I control, and
- publish a proper RSS feed so `garutyunov.com` can surface the latest posts.

## What's next

More posts on the things I build — hardware, firmware, tools, and the occasional
detour. Subscribe via [RSS](/rss.xml) if that's your thing.
